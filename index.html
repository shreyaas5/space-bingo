How to Play

Orbit
Mark all cells along the outermost border (edges) of your grid.
Then tap Claim Orbit.
Supernova
Mark all cells along both diagonals (corner to corner).
Then tap Claim Supernova.
Galaxy
Mark all cells in the inner 4×4 square (excluding outer edges).
Then tap Claim Galaxy.
Dipper (3×3 L)
Mark cells forming an L-shape: 3 down, 3 across anywhere on your grid.
(That is, a 3×3 corner with all cells in one row and one column within that 3×3 marked, forming an "L")
Then tap Claim Dipper (3×3 L).
Constellation (Any 3×3)
Mark any 3×3 square on your card as announced by the host.
Then tap Claim Constellation (Any 3×3).
Step 1: Tap the cells on your card as the host calls them.
Step 2: Once you complete a pattern, tap the corresponding claim button below your card.
Only correctly completed patterns will be accepted.
